Feb 2025 – present · Product Manager, Developer Experience · Greater Stockholm

## Problem

IFS engineering teams needed AI coding copilots as part of Developer Experience — tools and platforms that help internal engineers ship faster without inventing a separate product story.

## Approach

As Product Manager for Developer Experience at IFS, I set the strategy for the tools and platforms that empower internal engineering teams, global partners, and customers. One key pillar has been the rollout of AI coding copilots for IFS engineering teams.

## Outcome

AI coding copilots are a qualitative DevEx pillar for IFS internal engineering teams — aimed at efficiency and faster time-to-market for IFS offerings, without claiming measured copilots ROI here. The broader Developer Experience remit also covers global enterprise software used by millions of IFS Cloud / product users; that scale is the product footprint, not a copilots metric.
